# Project Brief

## Vision

Build a production-style legal analytics platform for litigation and eDiscovery teams. The system should turn large document productions into searchable, structured, and explainable investigation workspaces.

## Users

- Attorneys
- Litigation support teams
- eDiscovery professionals
- Legal operations and knowledge management teams

## Core Capabilities

- Document ingestion for PDFs, pleadings, motions, orders, contracts, email productions, chat exports, and native files.
- Metadata extraction for titles, file types, dates, senders, recipients, subjects, and custodians.
- Entity extraction for people, organizations, dates, currency values, locations, contract terms, and legal references.
- Document classification for contracts, NDAs, invoices, legal memos, internal emails, discovery responses, motions, and pleadings.
- AI-assisted search that combines metadata, full text, vector search, graph traversal, and citations.
- Analytics dashboards for document volume, file type distribution, custodian activity, timelines, and communication networks.
- AI evaluation for retrieval quality, extraction accuracy, classification quality, citation accuracy, and hallucination tracking.

## Guiding Principle

The platform should be an investigation environment, not a simple chatbot. Every AI output should be grounded in documents, citations, metadata, and measurable quality checks.
